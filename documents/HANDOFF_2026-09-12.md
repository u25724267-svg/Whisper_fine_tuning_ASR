## 4. Research objective and fixed protocol

The project studies limited-resource Shona ASR with multilingual Whisper.

- **RQ1:** static and dynamic acoustic curriculum learning.
- **RQ2:** controlled waveform augmentation and interaction with curriculum.
- **RQ3:** transfer of a Shona-selected curriculum to Tshivenda.
- **RQ4:** validation-calibrated proxy/hybrid-filtered pseudo-labeling.

The confirmatory Shona protocol is `waxal_shona_speaker_disjoint_v2`:

| Split | Rows | Hours | Speakers |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Train | 13,807 | 79.76 | 113 |
| Validation | 1,715 | 9.59 | 24 |
| Test | 1,671 | 9.56 | 24 |

There is zero speaker overlap. The held-out row/hour share is capped at 20% per
speaker.

The primary screen model is `openai/whisper-base` at revision
`e37978b90ca9030d5170a5c07aadb050351a65bb`. The fixed training contract is
three complete epochs, effective batch size 6, learning rate `1e-5`, 500 warmup
steps, FP16, deterministic execution, non-reentrant gradient checkpointing,
epoch-boundary evaluation/checkpoints at steps 2,302, 4,604, and 6,906, and
validation-only checkpoint selection. Decoding is greedy Shona transcription,
with forced/suppressed token lists cleared and maximum generation length 225.
